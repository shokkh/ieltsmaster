Vocabulary Builder

Expand your vocabulary with essential words for IELTS success

Academic
Profound
/prəˈfaʊnd/
Adjective: Having or showing great knowledge or insight; very great or intense.
"The professor's lectures had a profound impact on my understanding of the subject."
Business
Leverage
/ˈlevərɪdʒ/
Noun/Verb: The power to influence; to use something to maximum advantage.
"Companies can leverage social media to reach wider audiences."
Science
Hypothesis
/haɪˈpɒθəsɪs/
Noun: A proposed explanation made on limited evidence as a starting point for investigation.
"Scientists tested their hypothesis through multiple experiments."
Environment
Sustainability
/səˌsteɪnəˈbɪləti/
Noun: The ability to maintain or support a process over time without depleting resources.
"Environmental sustainability is crucial for future generations."
Technology
Innovation
/ˌɪnəˈveɪʃn/
Noun: A new method, idea, or product; the introduction of new things.
"Technological innovation has transformed how we communicate."
Academic
Paradigm
/ˈpærədaɪm/
Noun: A typical example or pattern; a worldview or model.
"The discovery led to a paradigm shift in scientific thinking."
